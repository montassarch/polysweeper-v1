Script: `lab/wallet_study.py` · Results: `lab/results/2026-10-04-wallet-study.json`.

## Summary for the owner

1. We followed 10 wallets that buy winners at 0.95+, over their **whole recent history** (up to 6,000 buys each, about 2-6 weeks), not just one week.
2. **Every loss we could time happened before the match was over.** After the end: **0 losses in about 3,900 buys.** But after the end, almost everything costs 0.999.
3. Warning sign: a wallet that looked perfect in last week's study (467j6yj, esports at 0.99) **lost 52 times and -$15,400** over its full month. Buying esports at 0.99 during play loses, even for big players.
4. **New lead:** the best wallet (0x4dDC…) buys at **0.99 in US sports** (baseball, American football, college football) near the end of the game: 1,344 buys, **3 losses (0.22%)**, **+$1,304** in two weeks. At 0.99 you can afford up to 1% losses, so this is a real edge, unlike our esports buys.
5. In US sports, 0.99 shares were still bought **right after the end** (28 buys, about 3,200 shares, by that wallet alone). **Our shadow mode does not watch US sports at all**, so we never saw this.

After the end the median price is **0.999** for almost everyone. The exception is 0x4dDC…: 0.99 in the first minute after the end of US games.

## What this means

- It confirms our rule: **only buy when the match is really over**. Every timed loss was a buy made before the end.
- Esports and tennis: after the end it's 0.999, a speed race (same as our end-window data).
- **US sports may be different**: slower and less crowded at the end, with 0.99 still available near and just after the end. This is the first sign of supply in our price range.
- Caution: a 0.22% loss rate is "rare", not "impossible". The owner wants near-zero losses, so the goal is to find **which game situations** gave 0x4dDC… its 1,341 wins (e.g. baseball 9th inning with a big lead) and only buy those, or only buy after the end.
