## Laptop analysis: can a "locked" check fix the price_only losses? (owner ask, 6 Oct)
Score at buy time for all 292 settled price_only buys (8 losses):
- Only 3 buys were made when the score already showed a decided match (esports 2-0 / 2-1, all won). The other 289 were mid-play, so a "locked only" check would leave almost no buys (same as the empty-book finding).
- No simple in-play score line separates the losses: esports 1-0 in Bo3: 53 won, 2 lost (96.4%, about break-even at 0.96); tennis losses at 1 set up 5-2, 1 set up 0-1, 0-0 5-5, 0-0 2-1, next to many wins in the same states; NFL lost with a 10-point lead.
- **Easy fix:** loss #1 (EAC Extra, Sep 30) was bought while Polymarket's own score said the other side had won (0-1, Bo1, ended). The score check's "against" verdict only logs; making it block buys would have saved $4.90.
- Conclusion: at 0.96 in play the market is priced about right, so price_only has almost no edge. Way forward: score veto that blocks + official-feed finish rule (recorder test) + tennis/esports "practically locked" rules only once there are hundreds of samples per state.
