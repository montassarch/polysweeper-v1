## Research

### Card 1 (#5): buy the proposed winner during the UMA check window
- Non-sports markets (politics, mentions, awards, economy) have a 2-hour window after a result is proposed. Sports use 15 min, esports 30-60 min, crypto 10 min. Tennis has 2 hours but is cleared in seconds.
- In 10 days, 51 of 319 top-volume markets had the winner trading at 0.985 or below inside the window, about 5 a day. Unlike sports, waiting buy orders at 0.98-0.99 did get filled. Total profit for all buyers combined was about $54 a day.
- Disputes: 18 of 16,692 markets. At least 2 were overturned after the eventual loser had traded at 0.95 or more. In "Anna Wintour" the loser traded at 0.999.
- Sources: https://docs.polymarket.com/concepts/resolution · https://www.polysyncer.com/blog/polymarket-resolution-time-2026 · `lab/results/2026-10-05-uma-*.json`

### Card 2 (#8): liquidity rewards and maker rebates
- About $156k a day is paid across 18,437 markets. Orders score by distance to the midpoint. Above 0.90 you must quote both sides. Rebates near 0.99 are almost nothing.
- For one minimum-size order on each side, the median is about $0.55 a day per market (a high guess). A near-zero-risk version was not found.
- Sources: https://docs.polymarket.com/developers/market-makers/liquidity-rewards · https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/maker-rebates.md · `lab/results/2026-10-05-rewards-scan.json`

### Card 3 (new, #18): become a paid UMA proposer
- Each market pays $0.6-5 to whoever proposes the correct result ($3.5-5 for non-sports), against a $250-500 deposit. Slow, thin markets can sit unproposed for about an hour. No trading and no speed race: we would read official sources.
- Only whitelisted proposers can do it: 5 or more proposals in 6 months at 95% accuracy or better. About 177 addresses do 96% of proposals today.
- Sources: https://blog.uma.xyz/articles/managed-proposers-update · https://www.theblock.co/post/366507/polymarket-uma-oracle-update

## Red team

- **#5: reject.**
  - Overturns do happen, including when the loser traded at 0.999, and through votes swayed by big holders (Ukraine minerals deal 2025).
  - 73% of fills happen in the first 30 min, so waiting for "no dispute yet" removes the trades.
  - One overturn at 0.99 wipes out about 100 wins.
- **#8: reject.** Quoting both sides means one side always gets picked off when news hits. One bad fill wipes out months of rewards.
- **#18: test with guards, shadow only.**
  - First blocker: getting on the whitelist. Polymarket's markets are whitelist-only now, so the 5 qualifying proposals must come from other UMA markets.
  - Losses come from ambiguous wording, proposing too early, late clarifications and bad-faith disputes. The deposit is about 70-100 rewards, so the break-even error rate is about 1-2%.
  - Guards: binary markets with one objective source only; propose 30-60 min after the deadline; skip markets with clarifications or disputes; skip when the price disagrees; at most 3 deposits at once.

## Afternoon: after-result study (laptop session, owner's request)

Question: once a result is certain, is anything left for us to buy, and is the PC fast enough?
Data: 154 end-window watches with the live feed (Oct 3 19:32 to Oct 5 11:44 UTC), 19 cut by restarts left out.
Time 0 = the moment shadow mode saw the result (Polymarket score decided, or ended flag).

- **Nothing for sale after we see the result.** Asks of 5+ shares at 0.96-0.995: 5 of 154 matches (4 were the Overwatch BO5 false alarm). At 0.995-0.999: 0 of 154.
- **The market knows 1.5-2.5 minutes before Polymarket's score does.** Median times relative to time 0:
  - our band (0.96-0.995) emptied: -94 s (10% of matches: -150 s or earlier);
  - late band (0.995-0.999) emptied: -92 s;
  - a 0.99 bid appeared: -151 s (score rule), -248 s (ended flag).
- Trades still happen after time 0 (126 matches, ~2.5 M shares at 0.995-0.999; 13 matches, ~16 k shares at 0.96-0.995), but with no asks left these are fills against resting bids at the top (0.99+), which the 0.99 bots already hold.
- **Conclusion:** faster ordering will not help. The gap is the result source: Polymarket's score lags the real end by ~1.5-2.5 min. The only safe route is a result source that is at least ~2 min faster than Polymarket's score (official live feeds per sport), then a check that asks are still there. Without it, the confirmed/score rules will keep making 0 buys.
- Limits: time 0 is our detection time, not the true end, so "before the result" may include moments that were only nearly certain (match point, last seconds). Trade prices and sides after time 0 are not saved (only counts), so the 0.99+ reading is an inference.
- Next: (1) save price and side of each after-result trade (small logging change, V1 session); (2) per sport, find a free result source and measure its lag against Polymarket's score using the daily score log.

### How do other traders know first? (desk research, same afternoon)

- Fast traders read the result from sources faster than Polymarket's `score` field:
  - official data feeds straight from the venue or game server (Sportradar, Genius, GRID for esports; paid), about 3 s behind the real event;
  - esports game APIs (LoL, Dota 2 live stats; CS2 scorebots), reported profits of $200k+ in late 2025;
  - people at the venue or watching low-delay streams (TV streams run ~30-45 s behind).
- **Free lead to test first:** Polymarket's own Sports WebSocket `wss://sports-api.polymarket.com/ws`. No login, no subscription message, pushes `live`/`ended`/`score`/`status` (esports `finished`, tennis `finished`, US sports `Final`) for all events; match on `gameId`; reply `pong` to the server's `ping` every 5 s. GRID (official CS2, Valorant, LoL data) is Polymarket's esports partner, so this feed may carry it.
- Unknown: is the Sports WebSocket faster than the Gamma `score` field we poll? Test: a lab script logs both feeds plus the winner's book for a few hours and measures the lag, then compares it with the 1.5-2.5 min gap.
- Sources: https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/websocket/sports · https://grid.gg/resources/newsroom/polymarket-partners-with-grid/ · https://x.com/0xMovez/status/2008298059059343819 · https://www.quantvps.com/blog/automated-sports-betting-bots-on-polymarket

## Evening: "almost certain moments" study (owner focus #1)

**In plain words:** we checked every common in-play moment that still had shares for sale at 0.96-0.995. In nearly all of them the leader still loses about as often as the price says (1 in 12 to 1 in 70). So buying there has no edge, and it explains our 7 losses. Only one moment is truly safe: **baseball, ahead by 7 or more runs after the 8th inning.** It is very rare to find for sale in our band (about 3 games in 81), and the regular season is over.

**Step 1: where shares were for sale** (PC score log, 1.8 days, 271 matches; `lab/state_supply.py`)
- About 20-25 matches a day had the leader's ask in 0.96-0.995 for minutes, often with thousands of shares.
- The busiest situations:
  - tennis, a set up and 3+ games ahead in set 2
  - CS2, 1-0 in maps and at map point
  - LoL map leads
  - NFL big leads
  - tennis deciding set, 3+ games ahead
- None of our 7 losses was in these situations. But 1.8 days is far too little data to judge safety.

- Tennis retirements: when a player retires, the player who advances wins. Retirement while a set up and 3+ games ahead happened 1 time in 9,711 matches. That is a small extra risk.

**Red team**
- Break-even after fees:
  - 1 loss in 51 at 0.98
  - 1 in 68 at 0.985
  - 1 in 103 at 0.99
- **MLB 7+ after 8: shadow test with guards.**
  - Real supply in the "lead still 7+" state: about 3 of 81 games (4,700 shares), roughly $20-60 a month in season. The other 3 games in the count were cheap only while the trailing team was scoring.
  - Guards:
    - Use the free MLB Stats API live feed (Polymarket's score as a veto only).
    - Match by game ID.
    - Require a lead of at least 7 plus the runners on base.
    - Never buy if the price falls below 0.98 while our data says the lead is 7+.
    - Junk-book filter and a size cap.
- **NFL: reject.** Leads of 17+ entering Q4 lose 1 in 65. 21+ still lost twice (for example Colts 36-14 → lost 2022).
- **CS2: reject for now.** At map level the leader loses 46 in 7,411. A cheap ask at a 0.997-fair state most likely means our score is stale.

**Decision**
- Owner focus #1 answered: in-play prices are fair. There is no tennis or esports rule that is safe at 0.96-0.99.
- The only candidate is a baseball shadow rule (MLB 7+ after 8, with the guards above). It is tiny until spring 2027 apart from the playoffs.
- Next for V1: decide whether to add it as a shadow-only rule (code change, owner's OK). Optionally, get Sackmann's tennis point-by-point data from the laptop to check the tennis model against real matches.
