## Research (ps-researcher)

### Card 1: complete set (idea #1)
- **Mechanism:** buy YES and NO of one market for under $1 in total, then merge them back into $1.
- **Finding:** Polymarket has one shared order book per market. A "buy NO at p" order is also a "sell YES at 1-p". A combined price under $1 means the book is crossed, and the matching engine fills those orders against each other at once.
- **Data:** the YES ask equalled 1 minus the NO bid in 6,935 of 6,935 markets. Of 6,112 markets with both asks, the lowest sum was 1.001.
- **Sources:** https://docs.polymarket.com/concepts/prices-orderbook · https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/ctf/overview

### Card 2: multi-outcome basket (idea #2)
- **Mechanism:** where exactly one option wins, buy one YES of every option. The NO version uses the negRisk "convert" feature.
- **Traps:**
  - "Augmented" markets have hidden reserve slots and an "Other" option, so a basket of only the named options is not complete. Example: Maryland Governor, two candidates at 0.991.
  - The fee is paid on every leg: about 3.3 cents on a 3-way football market.
- **Data:** of 991 non-augmented events ending within 72 h, none had a gap after fees (best -0.85 cents). The only positive gaps were tiny and long-dated (+1.3 cents x 44 shares, pays out April 2027).
- **Context:** an academic study found about $40M of past arbitrage profit of this kind. Bots close these gaps now.
- **Sources:** https://github.com/Polymarket/neg-risk-ctf-adapter/blob/main/docs/index.md · https://docs.polymarket.com/developers/neg-risk/overview · https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03474

### Card 3 (new): weather "dead ranges"
- **Mechanism:** about 95 daily temperature markets (48 cities, highest or lowest temperature, split into 1°C or 2°F ranges). The day's highest temperature can only go up. Once the airport station has passed a range, that range loses for certain, and its NO pays $1 hours before settlement.
- **Settlement:** the NOAA station timeseries (fallback Weather Underground), in the station's local day.
- **Competition:** open-source bots already do this from airport weather reports.
- **Sources:** market rule text (gamma-api `description`) · https://github.com/BallesJr/polymarket-weather-edge · https://github.com/jattree/weather-edge

## Red team (ps-red-team)

- **Card 1:** reject. "Gaps" only show up in stale fields (Gamma prices, last trade) or in paused books, where you cannot trade anyway.
- **Card 2:** reject.
  - The legs are separate orders, so one cheap leg can fill while the rest is lifted by bots, leaving a naked position. One failed leg in 50 wipes out the edge.
  - At the 5-share minimum, fee rounding adds about 0.5 cents per leg.
  - The watcher is allowed only as a passive logger.
- **Card 3:** test with guards. New traps found in the rule text:
  - **"No data → lowest range wins"**, in 91 of 100 events. For highest-temperature markets, the lowest range is the first to look "dead", so never buy NO on it.
  - Readings can be revised (7-day hold for clearly wrong data), so require 2 reports past the range or a 2-degree margin.
  - Hong Kong reports to 0.1°C with no rounding rule, and some Asian cities settle on Weather Underground pages, so skip those.
  - °F/°C conversion at range edges needs a 1-degree margin.
  - Readings near local midnight and on daylight-time change days are risky.
  - Lowest-temperature markets: ranges below the current low are never dead before midnight.
  - Old listings still show "active", so only trade markets with a live book.

## Tests and numbers (ps-tester)

Data: 1,416 resolved temperature events (Sep 19 to Oct 3) with all their public trades, plus 4,170 closed events for resolution history. Scripts are in `lab/weather_*.py`; results are in `lab/results/2026-10-03-weather.md`.

- **Who loses money:** buyers of NO on the range that actually won: -$86k on highest-temp markets and -$15k on lowest. 94% of those shares were bought before 17:00 local. They were weather-forecast bets, not sure things.
- **Ranges 2+ below the winner (truly dead):** 2.1M shares bought, +$28k, 0 losses. But almost all of it was bought before the day's peak, while it was still a guess:

- **After 17:00:** about $7.50 a day of profit was taken by others before any guards. Most of it was on lowest ranges, where the price reflects the "no data" risk.
- **Resolution history (42 days):**
  - 6 events resolved to the lowest range against the price (Panama City, Aug 30 to Sep 1). This is exactly the "no data" trap, so the red team's guard is needed.
  - 2 disputes. No 50/50 results.
- **Live books (19:54 UTC):** of 171 ranges far from the favourite in cities past their cutoff, 170 had no NO sellers and 1 was at 0.999.
- **Station data vs price (20:09 and 20:13 UTC):** for 11 US airports, all 166 ranges already passed by 1°F or more had **no NO sellers in either snapshot**.
- **Basket rescan (20:00 UTC):** 990 events, 0 net positive after fees.
- **Verdict:** kill as a sweeper strategy. In weather, just like sports, the sure side is cleared at 0.999 the moment it becomes sure.
