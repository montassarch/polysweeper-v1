# Weather dead buckets: test results, 2026-10-03

Data: 1,416 resolved daily-temperature events, event dates Sep 19 to Oct 3 (15 days), all taker trades from the
data-api. Resolution history: 4,170 closed events, Aug 23 to Oct 3. Station local time from `lab/weather_cities.py`.
"NO-side buy" means a taker buying NO at 0.95-0.998, or a taker selling YES at 0.002-0.05.
Full numbers: `2026-10-03-weather-deadbucket.json`.

## a) Wins and losses by distance from the winning bracket (15 days)
- Highest-temp markets, bracket is the winner (dd=0): 3,680 buys, 88,395 shares, **-$86,255**.
- Every bracket that was not the winner won: about 217k buys, +$85k. Brackets 2 or more below the winner:
  77k buys, 2.1M shares, +$28.2k (about 1.4% per share).
- Lowest-temp markets: dd=0 lost 356 buys, 15,019 shares, -$14,752. All other brackets won, +$7.5k.
- Losses on the lowest bracket: 41 buys, 649 shares. Each one was a case where the lowest bracket was the real
  winner (Seattle H Sep 19, Busan H Sep 27, Sao Paulo, Wuhan, Madrid L).

## b) When the losing buys happened
Highest-temp losses by share count: 19% were made the day before, 38% between 00:00 and 14:00 local, 37% between
14:00 and 17:00, and 6% after 17:00. They are forecast bets, or bets that the high would not climb any further. Every
large loss after 17:00 had the same cause: the temperature kept rising in the evening, so the winner ended up above
the price favourite. Examples: Tokyo Sep 21, winner 26°C, favourite was 24°C at 20:00, 4,066 shares lost. NYC Sep 20
(723 shares) and Chicago Sep 22 (643 shares) are the same pattern. None of these losses was on a bracket below a high
that had already been observed.

## c) Resolution history (42 days)
- No 50/50 results. Every event had exactly 1 winner.
- Disputes: 2 events. Amsterdam H Sep 7: 4 dead lower brackets were each disputed twice, and all resolved NO.
  Manila L Sep 18: the winning bracket went through 8 dispute entries.
- 84 events resolved to the lowest bracket. **6 resolved to it against the price**: Panama City H and L on Aug 30,
  Aug 31 and Sep 1. All 6 closed together on Aug 31 at 21:3x UTC, so the Sep 1 events closed before their day had
  even happened. This matches the "no data, lowest bracket" rule. Here a NO on a "dead" lowest bracket lost.
  Weaker flags: Denver L Sep 16 (cold front, probably real weather), Jinan L Sep 14 and Qingdao L Sep 14 (thin).

## d) Timing: do prices lag reality?
NO buys at 0.95-0.995 on brackets 2 or more below the winner, Highest-temp markets, by station local hour (15 days):
| local hour | shares |
|---|---|
| before the event day | 509k |
| 00-10 | 437k |
| 10-12 | 193k |
| 12-14 | 234k |
| 14-16 | 84k |
| 16-17 | 7.4k |
| 17-18 | 3.1k |
| 18-20 | 0 |
| 20-24 | 2.0k |
| after the day ends | 8.4k |

After 17:00 local there were 12 cases in 15 days, with $183 of taker edge in total. $71 of that was on lowest
brackets while the market waited for resolution (Paris Sep 24, Taipei Sep 28, Qingdao), where a price of 0.98-0.99
reflects the no-data rule risk. Without those: **about $112 in 15 days, about $7.5 a day**, and it is lumpy (NYC Sep 20,
Madrid Sep 21, Tokyo Sep 21). After 17:00, almost all trading on these brackets happens at 0.999 or higher, or not
at all.
Test that a bot could actually run (bracket 2 or more below the price favourite at the time, after 17:00 local):
89 buys, 2 losses, 10 shares (Munich and Jeddah, Sep 25). Those 2 losses come from last-trade prices going stale, not
from weather.

## e) and f) Live snapshots, Oct 3 at 19:54 and 20:10-20:13 UTC
- Events past 18:00 local (Highest) or 10:00 local (Lowest): 43. Of 171 brackets 2 or more from the favourite,
  170 had an empty NO book and 1 had its best ask at 0.999. Zero shares were offered at 0.998 or below.
- Observed max from aviationweather METAR (44 Highest events, 11 US stations): 166 brackets were already passed by
  1 degree or more (36 of them lowest brackets). **Every one had an empty NO book**, in both snapshots. The only cheap
  NO was on brackets the observed max had just reached (margin 0). Those are live brackets, not dead ones.
