# PolySweeper V1 — our bot (home)

Our own Polymarket sports "sweeper": buy the confirmed winner of a finished
match below $1, hold until it pays $1. Code lives in the repo's `code/` folder.

> Status (2026-10-07): **shadow mode running 24/7 on the owner's PC** (pretend trades only, no real money).
> Buying during play does not pay (+$0.35 on 370 trades). One question until 21 Oct: which method gets filled
> with zero losses? See [[34-Fill-Scoreboard]]. First real money: 1 Nov 2026, $20, only with the owner's yes.
