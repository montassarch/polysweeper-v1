# Legal and compliance

Back to [[README]] · Related: [[03-Risks]]

**This is not legal advice.** Check Polymarket's current Terms of Use and
your own local law before trading with real money.

From search summaries:

- Polymarket's terms **prohibit US persons** and persons in certain other
  jurisdictions from trading via UI **and API**, including agents built by
  people in restricted jurisdictions. Data is viewable globally.
- 40+ jurisdictions are restricted, mostly **close-only** (can exit, cannot
  open new positions). Examples reported: France, Germany, UK, Australia,
  Brazil, Singapore. **Fully blocked** (no new orders, no closing): Iran,
  Syria, Cuba, North Korea, occupied Ukrainian regions.
- Some restrictions apply to the frontend but not the API; do not rely on
  that as permission. The Terms of Use **prohibit circumventing geoblocks**;
  detection can put an account in close-only mode or close it.
- Read endpoints are not geoblocked, so research and dashboards work
  anywhere.
- Polymarket launched a **regulated US exchange and API** (2026) with its own
  fee schedule and KYC'd access; DIY bots built for the global venue do not
  automatically apply there.
- Taxes: trading gains are likely taxable in your country; keep a full trade
  journal (the bot's log doubles as this).
