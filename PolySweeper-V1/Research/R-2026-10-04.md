## Research

### Card A (new, #17): locked lines
- **Mechanism:** an MLB game has about 39 side markets and an NFL game about 315: totals, team totals, first-5-inning or first-half totals, and "run in the 1st inning?". Once the score passes the line, "Over" cannot lose, because runs and points can't be taken away once they stand. Polymarket settles these markets early: about 20 min after the lock in MLB, 45 min in NFL.
- **Wallet 0x4dDC… already does part of this.** Its "no run in the 1st" buys were all made right after a 0-0 first inning: 17 of 17 won.
- **Sources:**
  - Gamma rule text (e.g. mlb-cle-kc-2026-09-25 total and NRFI: cancelled = 50/50, postponed = stays open).
  - Play-by-play: statsapi.mlb.com live feed and the ESPN NFL summary.

### Card B (#15, deepened): our own win probability
- Tango's win-expectancy table (https://tangotiger.net/we.html) gives over 99.5% only in late, big-lead spots, such as top of the 9th, home team up 3 with 2 outs. Models are least reliable exactly there (https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18774).
- We rebuilt 0x4dDC…'s 100 MLB buys at 0.99:
  - 35 after the final out
  - about 45 in the 9th inning
  - about 20 in innings 7-8
  - All won, but the table puts many of them below 99%.

### Card C (#16): crypto Up or Down
- These markets settle on Chainlink's 60-second average price at the window start vs the window end. That is never certain by arithmetic: price can always move back.
- Data, 150 BTC 5-minute markets:
  - Our stand-in price source picked the wrong winner in 7 of 150.
  - The group of buys just next to the "safe" group lost 4.7%.

## Red team

- **A, test with guards.** Three ways to lose:
  - A game cancelled with no make-up pays 50/50. That happened at Mets-Marlins 2023 and Bills-Bengals 2023, and it is most likely on the last regular-season weekend.
  - Matching the wrong game: 1.3% in the raw scan.
  - A score shown live and then reversed. A backtest cannot see this, because the final play-by-play already has reversals removed.
  - Guards: game-start check, wait for the next pitch, or for the kickoff after the extra-point try.
  - The red team also found a counting error that tripled the "resting buy" supply.
- **B, park.** Even the safest spot (9th inning, 2 outs, lead of 4+) loses about 1 in 300-1,000. At 0.99 each loss wipes out about 100 wins.
- **C, reject.** It never locks, and the stand-in price disagreed with settlement 4.7% of the time.
