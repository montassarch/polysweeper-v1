**1. Programs, as of 8 Oct (docs read today; no fee, rebate or reward-rule change since 10 Jul):**
- **Liquidity rewards:** each market has its own daily pool. The book is sampled once a minute at a random moment; an order scores ((v - s)/v)^2 x size, where s = distance from the mid in cents and v = the market's max spread (usually 4.5c); only orders of at least the min size count (usually 20 shares; 50-1,000 on big markets). Mid between 0.10 and 0.90: one-sided quotes score 1/3; outside it, both sides are required. Your share = your score / everyone's score over 1,440 samples a day; paid at midnight UTC, minimum $1. An "in-game multiplier" exists but its value is not published; in March Madness in-play pools were 8x pre-game, and orders had to rest 3.5 s+.
- **Pool today:** $178,385/day over 18,351 markets (was $156k on 5 Oct). Top 100 markets get $44.7k, top 1,000 get $99.3k, and 10,810 markets get $1-5/day each.
- **Maker rebates:** makers pay no fee. Part of the taker fees goes back to the makers who got filled in that market, in proportion to filled size x p(1-p): sports 15% of a 0.05 fee rate, crypto 20% of 0.07, politics/finance/tech/mentions 25% of 0.04, economics/culture/weather/other 25% of 0.05; geopolitics has no fee and no rebate. At 0.99 this is almost nothing.
- **Holding rewards:** 3.25-4% a year (help centre and docs disagree) on positions in 600 eligible markets (2028 election 384, crypto "price before 2027" 108, midterms, data-centre and bankruptcy markets).
- **Sports:** every resting order is cancelled at the official game start; marketable sports orders are delayed (help centre: 3 s; 1 s on some leagues). Trap: `clearBookOnStart` is true on 272k of 280k open markets, so it is not a sports flag.
- **Not for us:** Perps pays $75k/day, but only to makers with 1%+ of 7-day maker volume.

**5. Honest $/day and worst loss.**
